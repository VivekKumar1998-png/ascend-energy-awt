# Ascend Energy V8: Knowledge articles (final text)

Fictional company. The structure follows what real utilities publish (PG&E due date extension, ComEd due date extension, Xcel Energy medical certification). Extension length is 7 days because the brief sets it.

The agent may quote only these articles. If a question is not covered, it says it doesn't know and offers a team member.
If you change `ExtensionDays` in the Flow, change Article 1 so both say the same number.

Each article has: Title, URL Name, Summary, Body.

---

## Article 1: Payment options

**Title:** Payment options
**URL Name:** payment-options
**Summary:** How to pay your bill, and what to do if you need more time.

**Body:**

You can pay your Ascend Energy bill in full at any time before the due date shown on your bill.

**If you need more time to pay**

- We can move the due date of your latest bill by 7 days.
- This can be done once per bill.
- We cannot extend a bill that has already been paid.
- We cannot extend a bill that has already been extended.
- We cannot extend a bill that is more than 30 days past its due date. A team member will review it with you instead.
- If your new due date has also passed, please pay as soon as you can.

**What an extension does not do**

- It does not remove or reduce any late fee. See the Late fee policy.
- It does not set up a payment plan or split the bill into instalments. A team member can help with this.

**Other ways to get help with a bill**

If you can't pay even with an extension, a team member can talk through other options with you.

## Article 2: Late fee policy

**Title:** Late fee policy
**URL Name:** late-fee-policy
**Summary:** When a late fee can be added, and what we can and cannot do about it.

**Body:**

**When a late fee is added**

A late fee can be added to a bill that is not paid by its due date.

**What the virtual assistant can and cannot do**

- It cannot waive, reduce or reverse a late fee or any other charge.
- It cannot promise that a fee will be removed.
- It can explain this policy.
- If you believe a fee is wrong, it can log your request so a team member reviews it. You will be given a case number.

**Extensions and late fees**

An extension gives you more time to pay your bill. It does not remove a late fee that has already been added.

**Bills more than 30 days overdue**

These bills cannot be extended. A team member will contact you about them.

## Article 3: Power outage guidance

**Title:** Power outage guidance
**URL Name:** power-outage-guidance
**Summary:** What to do during a power outage, and what to do if someone at home depends on medical equipment.

**Body:**

**If your power goes out**

1. Check whether your neighbours also have no power. If they do, the outage is probably in your area.
2. Switch off or unplug sensitive electrical equipment, such as computers and televisions. This protects it when the power comes back.
3. Keep fridge and freezer doors closed to keep food cold for longer.
4. Use torches or battery lights. Do not use candles.
5. Keep away from any fallen or damaged power lines. Treat them as live and call the emergency services.

**If someone at home uses medical equipment that needs power**

- Tell us immediately. We will treat your report as a priority and pass it to a team member.
- Keep a backup plan ready, such as a charged battery or somewhere else you can go.
- If anyone's health is at risk, call the emergency services first.

**What we cannot promise**

We cannot promise when your power will be restored. Outages can be unpredictable.
